The World System

Mount Meru, the seven golden ranges, the four continents and the twenty-eight realms above and below them, after Vasubandhu's Abhidharmakośa III and Jamgön Kongtrul's Myriad Worlds.

EastPūrvavideha — semicircular, crystal white
SouthJambudvīpa — trapezoidal, lapis blue
WestAparagodānīya — round, ruby red
NorthUttarakuru — square, emerald green

Each continent takes its colour and shape from the face of Meru it stands opposite, and is flanked by two subcontinents of the same shape and half the size. Radial distances and heights hold the textual figures at 1 m : 400,000 yojanas, with seven stated distortions — see Notes on the drawing in the index.
