# Artwork generation briefs

Built-in OpenAI image generation was used, with no external image inputs and
no copied source artwork. These briefs preserve the production subjects and
constraints. Requested output was 3072 × 2048; the delivered sheets are
1536 × 1024. Each sheet is a four-column, two-row asset atlas, not a mandala
placement diagram. No labels or numbers were requested within the artwork.

## Shared direction

Detailed classical Tibetan Menri thangka painting with natural adult anatomy,
fine mineral-pigment shading, delicate gold linework, silk drapery, turquoise
and coral jewellery. Serious and refined workmanship. Full subjects visible,
with small margins within their equal cells. No cartoon or geometric toy
figurines, European crowns, European medieval costumes or extra limbs. Each
figure or object rests on a small multicoloured lotus seat, except the celestial
discs. Do not copy any particular existing painting.
