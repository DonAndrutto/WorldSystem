# World System app icon

Created on 2026-09-11 with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool, using the
previous app icon as an identity reference. The selected artwork was resized
to a 1024 × 1024 high-quality WebP master. `scripts/build-icons.cjs` produces
the opaque PNG and ICO files; no image service is called by the app.

This is a symbolic emblem of Meru and its surrounding world, with blue and
white faces, gold ranges and turquoise channels. Its four prominent rings
abbreviate the seven ranges, much as the previous small icon abbreviated them.
The actual interactive model retains all seven ranges. The summit and terraces
are schematic, not a reconstruction of a specific traditional painting.

The regular icons use the full square artwork. The Android maskable icon
insets it to 82% and extends the blue edges, placing the coloured subject
inside the central 80%-diameter safe circle. The OS supplies the corner mask.
